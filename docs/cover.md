## Production prompt

```text
Create a refined premium editorial GitHub repository banner, wide 1536 by 1024 landscape composition. Entire design on deep midnight navy background with subtle technical grid, warm ivory and electric cyan accent palette. Left 48 percent is crisp highly legible oversized editorial typography reading exactly "Awesome" on first line and "3D Prompts" on next line in cyan, with small subtitle "Build games. Shape worlds." and a tiny footer "Curated by Tripo". Right half features an exquisite tactile isometric miniature creative universe: a floating circular island with a tiny futuristic observatory, a beautiful orange stylized fox character, miniature trees, a translucent blue fluid ribbon and a suspended geometric wireframe sphere. Transition from thin cyan wireframe on lower left of island to beautifully finished clay and glass materials on upper right. Museum quality 3D product render, realistic soft studio lighting, subtle cyan rim lights, restrained stars and orbital guides, immaculate sophisticated hierarchy and generous margins, no extraneous letters, no logos, no watermark, no button shapes. Title must remain readable when displayed at 800 px wide. Compose core content in central vertical 70 percent so it can also crop into a wide social preview.
```
